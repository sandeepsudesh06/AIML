Artifact 1 — AI Lab: Motivation Scenario

Introduction: This artifact presents an empathetic AI conversation that helps a user overcome fatigue and regain focus through motivational micro-steps.

Objective

  • Showcase user-centered prompt design principles.
  • Demonstrate the use of design thinking to refine tone and engagement.
  • Highlight clear documentation of AI interaction workflows.

Process

  1. Context: Identified target user scenario (fatigue + low focus).
  2. Design: Structured the flow — acknowledge → choose micro-task → confirm → reflect.
  3. Iteration: Adjusted tone and sequencing based on test feedback.
  4. Outcome: Created a reusable motivational conversation model for supportive AI design.

Tools & Technologies

  • ChatGPT (prompt design & iteration)
  • GitHub Pages (documentation)

Value Proposition

  • Demonstrates empathetic conversational design and tone control.
  • Balances clarity, trust, and motivation for real-world coaching applications.
  • Shows practical AI workflow presentation using a professional format.

Relevance

Relevant for roles in AI product design, data communication, or learning technology development.
