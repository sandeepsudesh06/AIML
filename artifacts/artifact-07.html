Reflection

For this portfolio artifact, I created a visual explanation of neural network structure and learning. This helped me understand how input layers, hidden layers, output layers, neurons, weights, activation functions, loss functions, and optimization algorithms work together. The process also helped me practice communicating complex AI concepts in a clear and professional way for both technical and non-technical audiences. Seeing the network architecture visually, with connections, weights, and data flow, made abstract mathematical concepts much more concrete and memorable.

Key Takeaways

Neural networks learn by passing data through connected layers, where each layer transforms the representation for the next stage. Weights, activation functions, loss functions, and optimization algorithms work together during training to minimize error and improve predictions. Visualizing neural networks makes complex AI concepts easier to understand, explain, and apply in real-world machine learning problems.
